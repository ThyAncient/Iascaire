*A psychological horror game set aboard a lonely fishing vessel, somewhere in the cold, fog-covered sea.*

## About the Game

You are a fisherman aboard a lonely vessel, carrying out your daily duties alongside the crew as you venture into the cold, fog-covered sea. But as strange noises begin echoing throughout the ship, something feels deeply wrong.

What begins as an ordinary night of fishing slowly turns into a terrifying struggle for survival. Investigate the strange occurrences, uncover what lurks beneath the fog, and survive whatever comes aboard the ship.

## Features

- **Atmospheric Horror** — Explore a fishing vessel surrounded by cold waters and thick fog.
- **Immersive Environment** — Experience the eerie atmosphere of life aboard an isolated ship.
- **Exploration and Interaction** — Carry out your duties and investigate unsettling occurrences.
- **Survival** — Uncover the mystery behind the strange sounds and whatever lurks in the darkness.
